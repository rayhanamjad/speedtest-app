What do these numbers mean?

Download

How fast you receive data. 25+ Mbps is good for HD streaming, 100+ for 4K & gaming households.

Upload

How fast you send data — video calls, livestreams, cloud backups. 10+ Mbps is comfortable.

Ping

Reaction time of your connection. Under 20 ms is excellent for gaming, under 50 ms is fine for most use.
